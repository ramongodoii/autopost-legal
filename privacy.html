AutoPost – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

AutoPost is a personal desktop application that runs locally on the user's own Windows computer and publishes videos chosen by the user to the user's own TikTok and YouTube accounts at times the user schedules. AutoPost has no servers, no website backend and no user database operated by the developer.

1. Information we access

When you connect your TikTok account, AutoPost requests only the following permissions:

  • user.info.basic – to show your display name inside the app so you can confirm which account is connected.
  • video.publish – to publish the videos you place in the app's videos folder to your own TikTok account.

AutoPost does not read your followers, messages, likes, comments, analytics or any other account data.

2. Where your data is stored

All data stays on your own computer (or USB drive) inside the AutoPost folder:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are saved in data/tokens/.
  • The posting queue and history are saved in a local SQLite file (data/queue.db).
  • Logs are saved in data/logs/.

None of this information is sent to the developer or to any third party.

3. Data sharing

AutoPost communicates only with the official TikTok API (open.tiktokapis.com) and the official YouTube Data API (googleapis.com), and only to authenticate you and upload the videos you selected. We do not sell, rent or share personal data, and we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

4. Data retention and deletion

You can remove access at any time by clicking Disconnect in AutoPost (which revokes and deletes the local token), by revoking the app in your TikTok account settings, or simply by deleting the AutoPost folder. Because nothing is stored outside your computer, deleting the folder removes all data.

5. Children

AutoPost is not directed to children under 13 and must be used in accordance with TikTok's and YouTube's own age requirements.
